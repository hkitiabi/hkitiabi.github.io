Teaching

Teaching and course design

I'm interested in teaching epidemiologic methods and biostatistics, One Health and digital health surveillance, infectious disease epidemiology (TB, HIV, and healthcare-associated infections), adolescent HIV prevention and testing, multilevel analysis of large claims data, and health equity and community-engaged public health practice.

Course design

POH 7117: Digital Health and One Health Surveillance (3 credits)

North Carolina State University · Advanced undergraduate/graduate course, one of four courses in a One Health series · 2026

  • Wrote the full syllabus: five learning outcomes aligned with Bloom's taxonomy, an eight-week scaffolded assessment plan, grading rubrics, and a plan for using technology in class (AI-based outbreak prediction, GIS mapping, and WHO Disease Outbreak News and WAHIS data).
  • Built active learning into every session: muddiest-point polling, tiered live polls, jigsaw case studies (COVID-19, Ebola, Lyme disease, and antimicrobial resistance), and peer assessment.
  • Designed and taught a microteaching session on outbreak investigation that used role-play and a peer-assessment rubric.

Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics, University of Georgia · Spring 2025
