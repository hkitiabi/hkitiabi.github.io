Research

What I work on

My main focus is Alzheimer's disease and related dementias (ADRD) and healthcare-associated infections in long-term care. I also work on infectious diseases more broadly (TB, HIV, and COVID-19) and on HIV prevention and testing among adolescents. On the methods side, I use multilevel and causal analysis of large administrative claims data. I'm also interested in One Health surveillance.

Current work

Postdoctoral Research Fellow, North Carolina State University

Rhea Epidemiology Lab, Department of Population Health and Pathobiology, College of Veterinary Medicine, Raleigh, NC · August 2025 – present

  • I'm the lead analyst on a multilevel study of ADRD and urinary tract infection (UTI) among Medicare skilled nursing facility (SNF) residents. The data, used under a CMS Data Use Agreement, cover about 1.85 million SNF stays in about 14,400 facilities and 3,144 counties.
  • I built the analysis pipeline in R. It links MedPAR, MDS, LTCFocus, Payroll-Based Journal, and CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index data through an SSA-to-FIPS crosswalk; defines ADRD (CCW and Bynum algorithms) and tiered UTI outcomes from ICD-10-CM codes; scores comorbidity with the Elixhauser method; and fits three-level mixed-effects logistic models in glmmTMB, with ICC decomposition, change-in-estimate confounder screening, and tests for effect modification.
  • I designed a machine-learning arm to run alongside the regression models: gradient-boosted trees with facility-grouped cross-validation, assessed for discrimination, calibration, and fairness across subgroups.
  • For the facility-level (LTCFocus) analysis plan, I set up a tiered system for flagging missing data and rules for when to use multiple imputation.
  • I'm leading a scoping review of 29 studies on ADRD and healthcare-associated infections, using a standardized extraction protocol organized by infection site.
  • Manuscripts from the main ADRD–UTI analysis are under peer review.
