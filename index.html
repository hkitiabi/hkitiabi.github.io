About

Infectious disease epidemiologist studying nosocomial infections and HIV prevention

I'm a postdoctoral research fellow in the Rhea Epidemiology Lab at North Carolina State University's College of Veterinary Medicine. My current work uses Medicare claims to study urinary tract infection among skilled nursing facility residents with Alzheimer's disease and related dementias, covering about 1.85 million stays in roughly 14,400 facilities.

I earned my PhD in epidemiology and biostatistics at the University of Georgia in 2025. My dissertation examined HIV risk, HIV testing, and PrEP awareness, willingness, and attitudes among US adolescents and young adults. Before that, I analyzed TB transmission data from Uganda, and my MPH thesis at Georgia State looked at TB mortality among children in Kenya who were living with HIV and had not started antiretroviral therapy.

I work mainly in R and also use SAS, SPSS, and NVivo. I also teach: I ran graduate epidemiology lab sections at UGA and designed a course on digital health and One Health surveillance at NC State.
